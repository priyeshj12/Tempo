        How it works

        Each friend's search is Dijkstra's algorithm on the road network. It spreads along the streets in order of distance, like water flooding the city.

        One by one: without knowing where everyone will end up, each search has to cover the whole map before the distances can be compared.

        All at once: the searches take turns, always growing whichever front is closest. The first junction every flood reaches is the place where the longest trip is as short as possible, so the search stops there.

        The lit streets around it are almost as fair, so pick whichever spot suits you. Places to go come from OpenStreetMap and are ranked by how far they are to walk.
