        How it works

        Each friend's search is Dijkstra's algorithm on the road graph: it spreads out along streets in order of distance, like water flooding the city.

        One by one: without knowing where everyone will end up, each search must cover the whole city before you can compare distances.

        All at once: the searches take turns, always growing whichever front is closest. The first junction that every flood has reached is the place that keeps the longest trip as short as possible, so the search can stop right there.
